# Film Journal: collection, watchlist and personal reviews

Film Journal has a dedicated collection, watchlist and personal journal. It supports manual film entry, personal ratings, reviews, watched dates, genre filters, sorting and JSON backups. It does not include task boards, notes collections or sales records.

Old Movies version 1 exports remain readable. New version 2 exports preserve watched dates. Importing an export replaces the current collection only after review; keep an export before restoring. Existing appearance and account cookie namespaces remain unchanged.

Cover treatments are decorative typography, not a licensed film catalogue. No external film ratings, streaming availability or official posters are inferred. Average ratings use watched films only. Watchlist films cannot carry a watched date.
